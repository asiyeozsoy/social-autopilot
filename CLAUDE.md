
## Asiye's standing rules (content)
- Never add music to videos (no generated lo-fi/piano, no Instagram library tracks). Export videos silent; Asiye picks trending audio herself in the app.
- Never turn image or carousel posts into reels. Posts stay posts; she adds music to carousels herself.
- Every video carries the brand logo (TinyNotes / Factyeap).
- Quality bar: only content that is genuinely funny or moving, very shareable, with a strong hook in the first second. Never reuse the same scenario (e.g. parents bickering / thermostat / "5-minute fix"); fewer, better posts over filler.
