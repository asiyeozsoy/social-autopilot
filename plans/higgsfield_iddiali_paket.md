# A · TinyNotes · "The Note" serisi (Veo 3.1)

**Sabit stil bloğu (her promptun başına):**
```
Cinematic vertical 9:16 short film, shot on 35mm, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, warm muted colors, gentle film grain, intimate and quiet mood, realistic people, subtle ambient sound only, no music, no dialogue, no on-screen text. The note is a small folded cream paper card with a tiny dark red heart drawn in the corner; any handwriting on it is soft and unreadable.
```

## note01 · Gece vardiyası
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: 3am hospital break room, an exhausted young nurse in blue scrubs drops onto a plastic chair and pulls a paper coffee cup closer. 2-5s: She notices a small folded cream note tucked under the cup sleeve, unfolds it slowly. 5-8s: Close-up of her tired face breaking into a small surprised smile, she presses the note to her chest and closes her eyes, fluorescent light humming.
- **Notta yazacak (ben eklerim):** "you're doing more good than you'll ever see."
- **Ekranda hook:** someone left this for the night shift
- **Açıklama:** for the ones who take care of everyone else 🤍 send this to a nurse, a doctor, a caregiver you love.

## note02 · Babaannenin cebi
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: An elderly woman in a cardigan sits alone by a rainy window, slowly putting on her old wool coat to go out. 2-5s: She puts her hand in the coat pocket and finds a small folded cream note, her glasses slipping down as she reads. 5-8s: Her hand trembles slightly, she laughs softly and wipes one eye, rain on the glass behind her.
- **Not:** "we miss you every single day. call you sunday? — your favorite grandkid"
- **Hook:** she found this in her coat pocket
- **Açıklama:** call your grandma today. that's it, that's the post 🤍

## note03 · İlk gün
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: Morning, a small child at a school desk nervously opens a lunchbox, the classroom loud and blurred behind. 2-5s: Inside the lunchbox, on top of a sandwich, a tiny folded cream note; the child unfolds it. 5-8s: The child's shoulders relax, a big gap-toothed grin, they carefully put the note in their shirt pocket and pat it.
- **Not:** "you are braver than you feel. love, mom"
- **Hook:** what his mom hid in his lunchbox
- **Açıklama:** the lunchbox notes they keep forever 🤍 send this to a parent

## note04 · Mülakattan önce
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: A young man in a slightly oversized suit paces in an office hallway, checking his reflection in a glass door. 2-5s: He reaches into his blazer pocket for his phone and pulls out a small folded cream note instead, confused. 5-8s: He reads it, exhales, straightens his tie, a quiet confident smile as a door opens with warm light.
- **Not:** "whatever happens in there, I'm proud of you."
- **Hook:** right before his big interview
- **Açıklama:** someone needs this before their big day 🤍 send it to them

## note05 · Mesafe
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: A young woman unpacks a suitcase alone in a tiny new apartment far from home, empty walls, city lights outside. 2-5s: Folded between her sweaters she finds a small cream note; she sits on the floor to read it. 5-8s: She laughs and cries at the same time, then sticks the note on the empty wall with tape, the first thing on it.
- **Not:** "home is wherever you are. we're one call away."
- **Hook:** she found this while unpacking
- **Açıklama:** for everyone who moved far from home 🤍 send it to the friend who did

## note06 · Kötü gün
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: Rainy bus stop at dusk, a tired woman stands under the shelter, shoulders slumped, phone at 2% battery. 2-5s: She notices a small folded cream note taped to the shelter glass at eye level; she hesitates, then takes it. 5-8s: She reads it, looks around at strangers, and quietly smiles for the first time, the bus headlights washing over her face.
- **Not:** "if today was heavy, put it down for a minute. you made it here."
- **Hook:** a stranger left this at the bus stop
- **Açıklama:** be someone's bus stop note today 🤍

## note07 · Sessiz baba
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: Early morning, a teenage girl grabs her car keys from a kitchen counter, a quiet father in a work jacket drinking coffee with his back to her. 2-5s: In the car she finds a small folded cream note on the steering wheel and opens it. 5-8s: She looks back at the house; through the kitchen window her father lifts his mug slightly without turning around; she smiles and starts the car.
- **Not:** "drive safe. text me when you get there. (I know you hate when I say that)"
- **Hook:** my dad doesn't say much. but
- **Açıklama:** the quiet ones love the loudest 🤍 send this to your dad

## note08 · Kapanış · Not zinciri
**Prompt:** [stil bloğu] 0-2s: Close-up of hands writing on a small cream note at a café table, then sliding it under a stranger's coffee saucer. 2-5s: Quick sequence: the stranger finds it and smiles, later that person leaves a new note in a library book, someone else finds that one on a park bench. 5-8s: The last person, a teenager, takes a blank cream note from their pocket and begins writing, camera pulling back on a sunny street.
- **Not:** "pass it on."
- **Hook:** one tiny note. watch what happens
- **Açıklama:** this is what we're doing here 🤍 who are you sending yours to?
