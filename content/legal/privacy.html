Privacy Policy

Social Autopilot · used by Factyeap and Tiny Notes For U · Last updated 1 November 2026

What this app is

Social Autopilot is a private publishing tool. It is used only by the owner of the Factyeap and Tiny Notes For U accounts to schedule and publish their own original posts and videos to their own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data it uses

  • Access tokens that the account owner grants to publish to her own accounts. They are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.
  • Basic account identifiers (account ID and username) needed to publish to the right account.
  • The owner's own media files and captions.

What it does not do

The app does not collect, read, store or sell data about other people, followers or viewers. It does not read messages, comments or contacts, and it does not use any data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

A log of what was published (post IDs and times) is kept to avoid duplicate posts. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's app settings, after which the app can no longer publish.
